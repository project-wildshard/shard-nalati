> Written by: Claude (opus-nalati2), 2026-11-22.

The population habitat (`trees`, `tops` in physicsRows.json: herds and
weather shelter) was still the 155-tree capture from before the page's
planting moved. The forest's capsule solids were re-captured from the
page in SS10b (146 trees), so `forest-trunks.mjs` now writes the habitat
from the same trees: [x, z, r] and the top height per tree.
`--from-bake` derives it from the captured capsules with no browser. The
format has taken up to 4,096 trees since G1004's pin of 155 went.
